How Score Tracking Works

Score Tracking turns a practice run into a measurable test. Over 20 rounds you mark each sense as a hit or a miss, then see how far past pure chance you landed.

🎯 Turning it on

On the home screen, tap the Score Tracking row. It switches to On — left = wrong, right = correct. With it left off, the app simply reveals each answer so you can practice freely with no scoring.

How a round works

  1. Pick a mode and settle in. Sense the hidden answer first, eyes closed if you like.
  2. Tap anywhere once to reveal. The app says the answer out loud and shows it on screen.
  3. Now record how you did by tapping one half of the screen:
← Left halfYou were wrong (a miss)
Right half →You were right (a hit)

It moves straight to the next round. The bar across the top shows your running score and the round number, both out of 20.

On a computer you can use the keyboard instead: Space to reveal, then ← for wrong or → for right.

Reading your score

Each mode has different odds, shown on its card. Pure guessing would land near these averages over 20 rounds:

  • Black or White  (1 in 2)about 10 / 20
  • Suits  (1 in 4)about 5 / 20
  • Dice  (1 in 6)about 3 to 4 / 20
  • Colors  (1 in 7)about 3 / 20
  • Deck of Cards  (1 in 52)under 1 / 20

Landing consistently above the chance average, across many sessions, is the real sign your intuition is doing more than guessing. One high session is not proof on its own. Watch the trend over time.

At the end

After 20 rounds you get your score out of 20 and a short message. Tap Try Again for another run in the same mode, or Home to switch modes.

Answers are spoken aloud, so turn your volume up. On iPhone, switch the side ringer on or you will not hear them.
